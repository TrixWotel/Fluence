# Fluence: English

Served at https://trixwotel.github.io/Fluence/english/ as the english folder of the Fluence site (see the space branch).

* **index.html** is the page: a way into the steps, and a way into writing.
* **learn.html**, **learn.js** and **steps.js** are How essays work: 14 short steps in five units, each with one idea, things to try (sort, put in order, match, tap the sentence, say what each sentence is doing, choose) and something to write yourself, then compare with an example. What you've done goes with the rest of your progress, as fluentio.essays.learn.
* **write.html** and **write.js** are the writing room: pick a prompt or bring your own question, plan it, write it (saved as you type, with a word count and a clock), and check it. Essays go with the rest of your progress, as fluentio.essays.list.
* **checks.js** is what the page can check by counting and looking for patterns: the shape of the essay, what reads as the thesis, the first sentence of each paragraph to read on their own, where the evidence and the explaining are, quotations left standing alone, tired phrases and filler. Nothing is sent anywhere and there's no AI, so it can't say whether the argument is any good. That's the rubric, also in this file, where you mark your own essay.
* **prompts.js** is what there is to write about: argument, poems and a speech from Macbeth, a made up story and speech, made up sources, and short paragraph tasks, each with a model essay marked up to show its parts. The models are written for this course. Their facts are real, and anything made up says so. The poems and the play are out of copyright.
* **english.css** is how it all looks. The colors come from the site's theme.

A push here asks the space branch to deploy, which puts the whole site together.
