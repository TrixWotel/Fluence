# Fluence: History

Served at https://trixwotel.github.io/Fluence/history/ as the history folder of the Fluence site (see the space branch).

* **index.html** is the page: the chapters first, then the notes, practice and tests, with the timeline next to the test.
* **chapters.html**, **chapters.js**, **chapters.css** and **chapters-home.js** are the chapters. A course is a list of chapters, and a chapter is a story where you make the choices, things to try (Myth or True, Who Am I, Match, Timeline, Sort) and a quick check of four questions of different kinds. How far you've got is kept with your progress, and three right in the check counts its topic as learned.
* **stories-middle-ages.js** and **stories-wars.js** are the stories, and what the things to try need, one for each topic.
* **kinds.js** makes a course's practice questions from its facts and stories, asked twelve ways.
* **middle-ages-data.js** is what the questions are made from: 14 topics from the fall of Rome to the printing press, each with its dates, questions with choices, names and words to type, pairs to match and sets to choose from. Every date is the usual one in school histories.
* **middle-ages.js** is the practice: 20 variants a topic (280), asked the twelve ways (myth or true, who am I from clues, sort onto two sides, put events in order, match, choose all that fit, the odd one out, true or false, the year, the century, and choosing or typing an answer).
* **middle-ages-exam.js** is the tests like the ones in class: key words and dates, a source to read and question (made up for the test, and it says so), and an answer to explain, marked against a mark scheme.
* **middle-ages-lessons.js** is the learning path (five units in time order, a lesson for each topic) and the timeline. A topic is learned after 4 right in a row.
* **wars-of-religion-data.js**, **wars-of-religion.js**, **wars-of-religion-exam.js** and **wars-of-religion-lessons.js** are the same for the Wars of Religion: 5 topics from Luther to the Peace of Westphalia, 100 practice questions, tests like the ones in class, and notes.

* **us-history-data.js**, **stories-us-history.js**, **us-history-lessons.js** and **us-history.js** are American History: 29 topics from the first peoples to the end of the Cold War, in eight units, with a chapter for each, 1,740 practice questions (three rounds of each topic's twelve ways) and notes. The facts, stories and notes are written in five parts in the **us** folder, and the files above put them together.

The test runner is in the space branch's kit folder. The colors and the rest of the look come from its settings.js.
